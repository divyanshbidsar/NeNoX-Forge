UI/UX Designer & Product Lead

Role 05 • Design Systems & Product Visuals

Professional toolkit: Figma, Interactive Prototyping, Design Systems, Motion Graphics, Wireframing

The UI/UX designer and product lead shapes how the product looks, feels, communicates, and guides people toward successful outcomes. The work starts with understanding users, goals, tasks, environments, accessibility needs, and points of friction. The designer explores structure through sketches and wireframes, tests important flows with interactive prototypes, and develops high-fidelity screens that communicate the intended experience to users and engineers. This role creates visual hierarchy, improves clarity, collaborates on scope, and keeps the product usable across devices. As product lead, the designer connects design decisions to product goals, prioritizes valuable experience improvements, and contributes to the final presentation narrative. Professional UI/UX is not decoration added at the end; it is a continuous process of understanding, testing, communicating, and refining the product.

Tools and key capabilities

  • Figma — supports collaborative interface design, components, variables, prototypes, documentation and handoff.
  • Wireframing — explores information architecture and interaction patterns before visual detail becomes expensive.
  • Interactive prototyping — simulates important journeys and reveals usability problems early.
  • Design systems — define components, typography, color, spacing, states and accessibility guidance.
  • Motion graphics — communicate hierarchy, feedback, transitions and system status with purpose.
  • User testing — gathers evidence from representative users and identifies friction in key flows.
  • Accessibility review — checks contrast, keyboard navigation, readable structure and inclusive interaction.
  • Design critique and handoff — align decisions with product goals and give engineers practical specifications.
