Our Ethos & Operating Style

We are building a dedicated team of self-driven students to compete—and win—in hackathons all year long[cite: 6]. We operate as an autonomous, independent student collective without micro-management, progress chasing, or corporate bureaucratic fluff[cite: 6, 8]. We expect builders who show up, collaborate, take initiative, and execute hyper-fast on their own[cite: 6, 11].

When you hit a wall or face a tough technical challenge, our experienced lineup has your back with mentorship and strategic guidance[cite: 6]. However, the internal fire to show up and build must originate from you[cite: 6].

Caution: We work aggressively ( Hyper Fast )

Governance & Core Guidelines

  • Independent Collective: NeNoX Forge is a self-organized student group and not an agent or official entity of any college or university[cite: 8, 11, 15]. Members operate strictly at their own individual legal risk[cite: 8].
  • Strict Infrastructure Limits: Security research, testing, or usage of security tools (such as Nmap, Metasploit, Wireshark, or Burp Suite) must occur strictly within authorized hackathon targets or isolated testbeds—never against university networks, portals, or campus Wi-Fi[cite: 4, 11].
  • Coursework Separation: Our Discord, GitHub organizations, and drives are reserved for building hackathon projects and must never be used for trading coursework or academic dishonesty[cite: 11, 20].
  • Prize & IP Ownership: Cash prizes, bounties, and grants belong strictly to the individual project team members based on pre-agreed splits[cite: 12, 20].
